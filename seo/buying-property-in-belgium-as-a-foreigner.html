Finding the right home

Housing choices vary by neighbourhood, property type, condition, transport access and budget. Use this page as a starting point for comparing options and questions to ask before contacting an agent or owner.

What to compare

  • Location, transport and everyday amenities
  • Floor area, bedrooms and outdoor space
  • Purchase or rental costs beyond the advertised price
  • Energy performance and expected maintenance
  • Documents, ownership details and applicable restrictions

For international residents

If you are moving to Belgium from abroad, allow time for document checks, financing, registration and local administrative requirements. Requirements can differ by situation and region, so verify important details with the relevant authority or a qualified professional.
