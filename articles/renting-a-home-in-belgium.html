Renting a home in Belgium: a practical first checklist

A good rental search is about more than monthly rent. Compare the full living arrangement before you commit.

Check the full monthly picture

Look beyond the advertised rent and ask which charges are included, which are separate, and how utilities are handled.

Read the lease carefully

Check duration, notice provisions, deposit arrangements, repair responsibilities and special conditions. Rental rules can differ between Belgium's regions, so use region-specific official guidance when you need a legal answer.

Document the condition

Before moving in, keep a detailed record of the property's condition and any agreed repairs.

Think about practical logistics

Check internet availability, heating, transport and everyday services before signing. If the home will be your main residence, also clarify the administrative steps that apply to your situation.

Compare the shortlist

Save a small shortlist and compare rent, charges, size, energy performance, transport, condition and lease terms.

Belgian FPS Finance provides MyRent information for registering certain residential leases and related documents. The exact obligations depend on the property and lease.
