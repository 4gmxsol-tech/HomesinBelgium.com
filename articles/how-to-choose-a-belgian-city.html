How to choose the right Belgian city for your lifestyle

The right city is rarely just the one with the most attractive homes. Start with the life you want to build, then let the property search follow.

Start with your weekly routine

Write down the places you will visit repeatedly: work, school, family, groceries, healthcare, sport and social activities. A beautiful home becomes much less convenient when every ordinary errand requires a long journey.

Compare the housing trade-off

Look at the type of home you actually need rather than searching by city name alone. An apartment, townhouse and detached home can create very different budgets and maintenance responsibilities even within the same area.

Think about pace, not just attractions

Visit neighborhoods at different times of day and pay attention to noise, walking routes, green space, local shops and how easily you can get around.

Shortlist three places

Pick one obvious candidate, one alternative with a different lifestyle and one place that challenges your assumptions. Then compare the same criteria for all three.

Only then start comparing listings

Save comparable homes and record price, size, condition, location and travel time. Patterns become much easier to see when you compare like with like.
