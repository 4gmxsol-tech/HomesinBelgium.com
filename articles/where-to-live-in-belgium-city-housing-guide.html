CITY & LOCATION GUIDE

Where to Live in Belgium: Compare Cities for Housing

The right Belgian city depends on your work, budget, language needs and preferred lifestyle. Compare neighbourhoods and real commute times rather than choosing by reputation alone.

Brussels: international work and transport

Brussels may suit people working with international organisations, companies and public institutions. Compare neighbourhoods for transit access, noise, green space and the journey to your workplace.

Antwerp: a major city with varied districts

Antwerp offers urban amenities and strong connections to business and transport networks. Check the route you would use every day and compare the character and housing options of individual districts.

Ghent: university and cultural life

Ghent can appeal to students, researchers and people who enjoy a lively historic city. Consider cycling infrastructure, train access and demand in areas close to your university or job.

Leuven: study and research

Leuven is worth comparing if you work or study in the university and research ecosystem. Begin a rental search early when your move is tied to the academic year, and check the complete lease terms.

Bruges: historic surroundings

Bruges offers a distinctive historic setting. Think about whether your daily routine is centred on the city itself or requires regular travel to another town or employment hub.

Liège: an urban base in Wallonia

Liège is a major Walloon city. Language preferences, local services, transport and the locations of your work or study should all inform your choice.

Compare more than the asking price

  • Monthly rent or total purchase budget
  • Energy rating and likely utility bills
  • Commute at the times you will travel
  • Public transport, cycling and parking
  • Access to schools, shops, healthcare and green space
  • Lease conditions or likely renovation needs

City-wide averages can hide major differences between neighbourhoods. Compare current listings with similar property types and sizes, and visit the area if possible.
