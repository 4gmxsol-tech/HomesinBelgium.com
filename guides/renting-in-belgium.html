Renting a home in Belgium

A clear starting point for comparing rental options and preparing for a tenancy.

Advertisement

Compare the total monthly cost

Look beyond headline rent and check which utilities, charges, insurance and other recurring costs apply.

Prepare your documents

Have the information commonly requested by landlords or agents ready, while checking what is legally required in your situation.

Inspect before signing

Review the property's condition and the tenancy documents carefully. Ask questions about repairs, deposits and notice terms before committing.

Choose the location, not just the property

Consider public transport, daily services, work or study routes and the character of the surrounding neighborhood.
