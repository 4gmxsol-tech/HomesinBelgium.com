Buying a home in Belgium

A practical starting point for anyone researching the Belgian property market.

Start with your search

Define your preferred city, property type, budget and essential requirements before contacting agents or arranging viewings.

Understand the full cost

The purchase price is only one part of the budget. Buyers should research applicable taxes, registration costs, notary fees, financing costs and ongoing ownership expenses.

Do your due diligence

Before committing, review the property's documents, condition, energy information and any relevant restrictions with qualified local professionals.

Compare neighborhoods

Transport, schools, services, noise, green space and commute times can materially affect the suitability of a property.
